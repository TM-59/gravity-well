Both masses move around their common centre. This lab uses total GM = separation = angular frequency = 1. It is a separate model from the fixed-star flight and Ice Run. L4 and L5 are linearly stable only while μ is below Routh's limit 0.03852; L1–L3 are saddles at every μ. The inertial view shows the same C trajectory without rotating with the pair.

The point masses are singular. The run stops at 60 T or before a step exceeds |ΔC| = 1e−6. An open neck permits passage; it does not promise one.
